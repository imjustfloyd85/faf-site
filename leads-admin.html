Cold Call Script

▼

Opener

"Hi, this is Justin/Coach Floyd calling from Fathers and Football, we run a youth flag football program based here in [Frisco/McKinney/Allen] — our travel team Frisco Elite just came off a strong season, we were 2025 Dallas Cowboys Regional Champions and we've actually been invited to play in the Battle Brand Texas Regional this coming March. Do you have two minutes? I'm calling around to a few local businesses about a partnership for this season."

If they say no or seem busy: "No worries at all — is there a better time I could call back, or is there someone else on your team who handles community sponsorships?"

The hook (why this matters, keep it short)

"We're a registered 501(c)(3), and everything we do is about giving kids in this area a real, positive experience playing football — good coaching, real competition, and a program parents trust. We've got kids going to national-level tournaments this year, and the community support is a big part of how we get there."

The actual ask

"What we're raising money for this season is pretty specific — it's not just 'help us out.' We need it for:
  • Player evaluation and tracking data, so we can actually show kids and parents real development over the season
  • Group training sessions with real coaching, not just practice reps
  • Liability insurance, which isn't optional for us
  • Cameras so we can film games and practices for film study and highlight reels
  • Uniforms and game equipment — balls, etc.
That's real, concrete stuff, not overhead. A sponsorship gets your name in front of the families in this program, on our site, and at our events — depending on the level, that's jerseys, banners, site placement, that kind of thing."

If they're interested

"That's great to hear. What I'd love to do is send you over something in writing — a quick one-pager on the tiers and what each one includes — and then we can find 10-15 minutes to actually talk through what makes sense for you. What's the best email for that?"

If they want to think about it / aren't sure

"Totally fair, no pressure at all. Would it help if I sent over some information first, so you can look at it whenever works for you? Even if a full sponsorship isn't the right fit right now, we're always open to whatever level makes sense — some folks do a smaller boost, some do a full season partnership."

If they say partner instead of sponsor

Note the distinction for your own tracking, not necessarily for the call itself:

  • Sponsor = they're giving money/product in exchange for visibility (logo, banner, jersey, site placement) — a transactional relationship.
  • Partner = a deeper relationship — providing a facility, ongoing services, co-hosted events, something beyond a one-time or seasonal payment (this is what Athlete Training and Health is to FAF).

If a business seems more interested in an ongoing relationship than a line-item sponsorship, mark them as a potential partner lead, not sponsor, in your notes.

Close, regardless of outcome

"I really appreciate you taking the time. Even if this isn't the right season for it, I'd love to keep you in mind for next year, or if you ever want to just come check out a game. Thanks again."

Reminders for yourself mid-call

  • Note anything specific they mention (a kid of theirs who plays, a past sponsorship they've done, a specific concern) — write it down right after the call while it's fresh.
  • Rate how promising it felt honestly, not optimistically. A "maybe, call back in a month" is more useful data than an inflated "yes" that never closes.
