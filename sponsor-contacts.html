Portfolio-Level Blocker

FAF is 501(c)(3) pending. Most foundations below require an active determination letter before accepting grant applications. Several grants also require 2+ years of active nonprofit history (e.g., Dallas Mavericks Foundation). Getting the IRS determination letter is the single most valuable parallel track to sponsor outreach. Until it lands, focus on:

  1. Corporate sponsorship paths (deductible as marketing expense, no 501(c)(3) required)
  2. In-kind requests (auction items, ticket nights, equipment)
  3. Relationship-building with foundations for when FAF does have the letter
  4. Pledge commitments (see MONEY-HANDLING.md)
