Skills Clinic & Combine

A free skills clinic and light combine for 10U and 12U players (5th–7th grade), hosted by Fathers and Football.

Date
Saturday, September 13
Location
Athlete Training and Health, Allen, TX
Schedule
Check-in 6:00 AM · Warmup 6:30 AM · Ends 9:00 AM
Cost
Free
Ages
10U & 12U (5th–7th grade)
Registration Deadline
September 9

What to Expect

Players start with skills training. Based on performance, a group will be selected to run a light combine: laser-timed 40-yard dash, vertical jump, and broad jump.

Players not selected for the combine will be invited to our next one — this clinic isn’t a one-shot cut, it’s the start of the evaluation process.
