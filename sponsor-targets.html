Frisco Sponsorship Target List -- Internal

For Rob. This is the outreach playbook, not a polished handout. Verify contact names and addresses before reaching out -- titles and decision-makers change. Start with the warmest relationships first.

How to Use This List

  1. Start warm. Anyone Rob or Kellie already knows through Microsoft, MMA, church, school, or Frisco Elite family networks goes first. Warm leads close 10x better than cold.
  2. Tier the ask. Don't lead with Gold ($2,500) for a small business. Match the ask to the business size -- a local restaurant gets a Bronze or Silver ask, a car dealership or corporate office gets a Gold ask.
  3. Ask for the decision-maker. At small businesses, it's the owner. At chains, it's the local manager + corporate community relations. At corporates, it's community affairs, CSR, or employee-giving program lead.
  4. Track every touch. Use the outreach tracker below. Follow up in 3 business days. A "no" now is often a "yes" next season.

Weekly Cadence

  • Monday: Rob and Justin sync for 15 minutes -- review pipeline, pick the week's 10 targets
  • Tuesday-Wednesday: Send 10 personalized emails from the target list
  • Thursday: Follow-up calls on emails from 3 days prior
  • Friday: Log results, thank anyone who said yes, decide next week's list

Target close rate: 10% on cold, 40% on warm. If you hit 50 outreaches in 30 days with a good mix of warm/cold, you should land $5K-$15K in committed sponsorships.

Email Template (Use the FAF-STRATEGY.md version, personalize per target)

The core pitch is in FAF-STRATEGY.md section 4.3. For Frisco specifically, add one of these hooks:

  • Cowboys/Frisco sports angle: "Frisco is the youth sports capital of America, and FAF is building the fatherhood program this community needs."
  • Championship credibility: "Our Frisco Elite team took home the 2025 Dallas Cowboys Regional Championship, and we're building on that foundation."
  • Veteran/MMA angle (for veteran-owned or law enforcement businesses): Lead with Rob's Navy + MMA background.
  • Microsoft angle (for corporate prospects): Mention Rob's Microsoft role to establish credibility.

Do NOT Approach Yet

  • Any business until the bank account is open OR a fiscal sponsor is confirmed (see MONEY-HANDLING.md). You can collect pledges without an account, but not cash.
  • Businesses where Rob or Kellie has an active conflict (existing customer relationship where a sponsorship ask would strain the business relationship).
  • Any sponsor who wants 501(c)(3) tax deduction right now -- we can't guarantee that until the IRS determination letter is issued. Pitch them after that.
